## How films get in

Only films for a product or a service, with designed motion, checked by hand or by our review before they go up. Found through [awesome-claude-5-5-videos](https://github.com/athemeroy/awesome-claude-5-5-videos) (CC BY 4.0), our own search and what people send in; the titles, notes and tags are ours. Made one? [Send it in](https://omnigami.ai/gallery/submit).
